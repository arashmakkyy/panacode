## 1. Verdict

**Overall completion: about 20–25%** of what the prompt asks for.

What exists is a **foundation plus a CSS overlay**, not a full redesign.

**Done or mostly done:**
- A real `@panacode/ui` SolidJS package with Arc tokens copied verbatim from the reference, five primitives, and a Panacode theme.
- The canonical Switch look, applied through a CSS bridge.
- An `UPSTREAM-MERGE.md` with sections A–L.
- A static ownership guard.
- Everything typechecks and the test suites pass.

**Real integrations: only 2 of the 8 features are mounted in app screens:**
- **MCP Servers:** Settings → Extensions → MCP, and Project settings → Extensions → MCP.
- **Agent Approvals:** the permission dock in the session composer.

Both are wired to real OpenCode controllers. Both are very low-fidelity ports of the references.

**Unwired shells: the other 6 are exported but never rendered anywhere.** AI Chat, AI Composer, Agent Run, Agent Orchestrator, Tool Call and Code Suggestion are 33–67-line presentational stubs. Their adapters are pure status/shape mappers that no app code calls. They are not "fake data" (no mock data ships), but they are dead code. The Phase 1 claim that the eight features are integrated is not true.

**Phase 2 (full visual migration): about 10–15%.**
- It is one 188-line `shell.css` overlay scoped to `[data-brand="panacode"]`.
- Several of its selectors match nothing in the upstream DOM.
- No surface was rebuilt with Panacode components.

**Fidelity versus the references is low across the board:**
- Ports total about 450 lines of TSX and 360 lines of CSS.
- The references total about 27,000 lines of TSX/CSS/TS.
- Signature behaviours and animations are absent.

**Branding:**
- Splash, About, icons, wordmark, window title (app root) and Windows menu are Panacode.
- About **35 English UI strings still say "OpenCode"**.
- The session page title still renders `"{session} · OpenCode"` (`packages/app/src/session/route.tsx:293`).
